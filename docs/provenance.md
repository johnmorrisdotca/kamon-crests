# Provenance: how each crest is constructed

Every drawing is made for this package and is MIT. The geometric crests are struck from their construction with compass and ruler.
The organic crests whose source is given below are vectorised from a public-domain period crest book: the page was read at the
National Diet Library, the crest's outline drawing was filled, traced to curves, cleaned of print noise, and made exactly symmetric
by keeping one half (or one leaf) and mirroring or turning it; no scan, crop or picture of the book is in the package or the
repository. This is the one-line rule each crest is set out by, in English and in Japanese, and the page it was drawn from.
Written by `pnpm data`; do not edit it by hand.

## Rings

The weight of a ring and the white inside it are measured from fifteen ringed crests drawn in the usual way (fourteen in a sheet of
ringed crests and one ringed chrysanthemum, the reference pictures the package was checked against): the ring is 0.079 to 0.095 of
the crest's diameter, 0.087 at the median; the white between ring and motif is 0.011 to 0.029 of the diameter, 0.018 at the median;
the motif reaches 0.77 to 0.80 of the outer radius. So a medium ring is 16.8 on the 192 of the layout circle, the white inside it 3.5,
and a ringed motif is drawn to the ring's inside less that white. A thin ring is a thirtieth of the diameter.

## Three stars over one bar · 一文字三つ星 (`ichimonji-mitsuboshi`)

Three discs of one size set like the character 品, one above two, and under them a bar as long as the two lower discs are wide apart, all inside a ring of about a tenth of the diameter.

同じ大きさの円を三つ、品の字に並べ（上に一つ、下に二つ）、その下に、下の二つの円の幅と同じ長さの横一文字を引きます。全体を直径の約10分の1の太さの輪で囲みます。

4 guides, 5 shapes, 10 steps.

## Well frame · 井桁 (`igeta`)

## Snake's eye · 蛇の目 (`janome`)

## Left three-comma tomoe · 左三つ巴 (`hidari-mitsu-tomoe`)

On each of three spokes a third of a turn apart stand two circles that touch the outer circle from inside at the spoke's end: the head, 0.45 of the radius, and the swirl, 0.66. A comma is three arcs: the outer circle from the end of the spoke behind to its own, its head, and the swirl of the spoke behind. The white is three horns, each the crescent between a swirl and its head, and each tail thins to a point on the outer circle, which stays whole.

120度ずつ離れた三本の線の上に、線の端で外円に内側から接する円を二つずつ置きます。頭の円は半径の0.45、渦の円は0.66です。巴一つは三つの弧でできます。後ろの線の端から自分の線の端までの外円、頭の円、そして後ろの線の渦の円です。白い部分は三本の角の形で、それぞれ渦の円と頭の円の間の三日月です。尾は外円の上で細くなって尖り、外円は欠けません。

10 guides, 3 shapes, 14 steps.

## Sixteen-petal chrysanthemum · 十六八重表菊 (`juroku-yae-giku`)

The sixteen-division: sixteen petals packed edge to edge round a small centre disc, each petal's two sides parallel to spokes a sixteenth of a turn apart with a thin white line between neighbours, and each ending in a round tip, the circle that touches both sides; at the rim, between the front tips, show the round tips of sixteen petals behind, turned half a petal.

## Three ginkgo leaves · 三つ銀杏 (`mitsu-icho`)

## Three hollyhock leaves · 三つ葉葵 (`mitsuba-aoi`)

Three heart-shaped leaves a third of a turn apart, each with its point at the centre and its two round lobes outside, white stems between them. Every edge is an arc of a compass circle or a ruled line: each lobe is one circle, its straight side a line through two grid points, and its veins arcs and lines 1.7 wide that fork towards shared points on the edge. Between the two lobes a black spindle runs up the leaf's axis from near the centre and ends in a point at the notch; beside it the outline rolls into a curl with a hook.

## Crescent moon · 三日月 (`mikazuki`)

A circle with a second circle of the same size set a quarter of its width across it; what is left of the first is the crescent.

円に、同じ大きさの円を、幅の4分の1だけずらして重ね、最初の円の残った部分を三日月とします。

2 guides, 1 shapes, 4 steps.

## Oda quince · 織田木瓜 (`oda-mokko`)

A band whose outer edge is the outline of five equal circles set round the centre, with a flower of five round petals inside it.

中心のまわりに同じ大きさの円を五つ置き、その外形を縁取りとする帯を描き、中に五弁の丸い花を置きます。

11 guides, 6 shapes, 18 steps.

## Open fan · 扇 (`ogi`)

A fan opened to about a quarter of a turn, its paper between two arcs struck from the pivot, with seven ribs cut out and a stick at each edge.

要を中心に、4分の1回転ほど開いた扇を描きます。二つの円弧の間に紙を置き、骨を七本白く抜いて、両端に親骨を添えます。

4 guides, 4 shapes, 9 steps.

## Seven treasures · 七宝 (`shippo`)

Four equal circles, each centred one radius from the middle toward up, down, left and right, so that every one passes through the centre; each overlaps the next in a petal.

同じ大きさの円を四つ、中心から半径の長さだけ上下左右へずらして描き、どの円も中心を通るようにします。隣り合う円は花弁の形に重なります。

4 guides, 4 shapes, 9 steps.

## Four eyes · 四つ目結 (`yotsume-yui`)

Four equal square frames in two rows of two with a narrow space between, the whole turned an eighth of a turn onto its corner.

同じ大きさの四角い枠を二段二列に、狭い間隔をあけて並べ、全体を45度回して角を上に向けます。

1 guides, 4 shapes, 6 steps.

## Swallowtail butterfly · 揚羽蝶 (`agehacho`)

Seen from the side: a slanting body, a raised forewing with three eyespots, a fan-shaped hindwing with its tail, the far wing showing at the left, and the antennae curled; veins and eyespots are cut out of the wings.

横から見た姿を描きます。斜めの胴、三つの目玉模様のある立てた前翅、尾状突起のある扇形の後翅、左にのぞく奥の翅、巻いた触角を置き、翅に翅脈と目玉模様を白く抜きます。

3 guides, 10 shapes, 6 steps.

## Wisteria wreath · 藤の丸 (`fuji-no-maru`)

The same two clusters, begun nearer the top so that they round the circle almost to meet at the leaves.

同じ二房を、上の近くから始めて、葉のところでほとんど出会うように、円に沿って回します。

4 guides, 7 shapes, 7 steps.

## Nine luminaries · 九曜 (`kuyo`)

One disc in the middle and eight round it, each a fifth of the diameter across and a little apart.

中央に円を一つ、そのまわりに八つを、それぞれ直径の5分の1ほどの大きさで、少し間をあけて並べます。

9 guides, 9 shapes, 19 steps.

## Hanging wisteria · 下がり藤 (`sagari-fuji`)

Three leaves and the stalks' knot at the top; from them two clusters of florets hang down round a circle, each narrowing toward its end, the florets parted by curved white lines. The left half is drawn from the book and mirrored about the axis.

上に葉を三枚と花房の付け根を置き、そこから二房の花が円に沿って垂れ下がり、先へ向かって細くなります。小花の間は白い曲線で分けます。左半分を紋帳から写し取り、中心線で左右対称に返します。

## Bamboo grass and gentian · 笹竜胆 (`sasa-rindo`)

Five bamboo-grass leaves fanned below, the middle one upright with its midrib, and above them three gentian flowers, the middle one the largest, each a bell of five pointed petals. The left half is drawn from the book and mirrored about the axis.

下に笹の葉を五枚扇状に広げ、中央の葉は立てて中肋を入れます。その上に竜胆の花を三つ、中央を最も大きく、五枚の尖った花弁の鐘形に置きます。左半分を紋帳から写し取り、中心線で左右対称に返します。

## Tachibana orange · 橘 (`tachibana`)

A round fruit whose top is parted into segments round its calyx, a pointed leaf standing above it and a leaf to each side, two more leaves below, and a short stem. The left half is drawn from the book and mirrored about the axis.

丸い実の上部を蔕のまわりで房に分け、その上に尖った葉を一枚立て、左右に一枚ずつ、下にも二枚の葉と短い枝をつけます。左半分を紋帳から写し取り、中心線で左右対称に返します。

## Dragonfly · 蜻蛉 (`tonbo`)

A long body on the centre line, a round head, and two pairs of long wings spread across it, the lower pair a little shorter.

中心線上に細長い胴を置き、丸い頭をつけ、左右に長い翅を二対、広げます。下の翅はやや短くします。

1 guides, 8 shapes, 7 steps.

## Front-facing rabbit · 真向き兎 (`usagi`)

A rabbit crouched and seen from the front. Its body is two equal circles side by side, which cross on the axis and leave a notch under the chin, with two ruled lines from the point of the back touching them. Each ear is the lens between two equal arcs, leaning out. The face is a circle drawn as a white line, with ringed eyes, the line of the nose, four ruled whiskers a side, and an arm coming round to a three-clawed paw.

正面から見た、うずくまる兎です。体は同じ大きさの円を二つ並べ、中心線上で交わらせて顎の下に切れ込みを作り、背の頂点から二本の直線を円に接するように引きます。耳は同じ半径の二つの弧にはさまれた形で、外へ傾けます。顔は白い線の円で、輪の目、鼻筋の線、左右四本ずつの直線のひげ、三本爪の前足へ回る腕の線を入れます。

13 guides, 1 shapes, 15 steps.
