# Provenance: how each crest is constructed

Every drawing is made for this package and is MIT. The geometric crests are struck from their construction with compass and ruler.
The organic crests whose source is given below are vectorised from a public-domain period crest book: the page was read at the
National Diet Library, the crest's outline drawing was filled, traced to curves, cleaned of print noise, and made exactly symmetric
by keeping one half (or one leaf) and mirroring or turning it; no scan, crop or picture of the book is in the package or the
repository. This is the one-line rule each crest is set out by, in English and in Japanese, and the page it was drawn from.
Written by `pnpm data`; do not edit it by hand.

## Rings

The weight of a ring and the white inside it are measured from fifteen ringed crests drawn in the usual way (fourteen in a sheet of
ringed crests and one ringed chrysanthemum, the reference pictures the package was checked against): the ring is 0.079 to 0.095 of
the crest's diameter, 0.087 at the median; the white between ring and motif is 0.011 to 0.029 of the diameter, 0.018 at the median;
the motif reaches 0.77 to 0.80 of the outer radius. So a medium ring is 16.8 on the 192 of the layout circle, the white inside it 3.5,
and a ringed motif is drawn to the ring's inside less that white. A thin ring is a thirtieth of the diameter.

## One bar over three stars · 一文字に三つ星 (`ichimonji-mitsuboshi`)

The character 一 as the brush writes it, over three discs of one size set like the character 品, one above two. The stroke's top is a ruled line and its underside a wide arc; its left end is a circle as large as a disc, cut slantwise above, and its right end a smaller circle with a slanting line under it; the top rises to a point at each end, in a hollow arc.

筆で書いた「一」の字を上に置き、その下に同じ大きさの円を三つ、品の字に並べます（上に一つ、下に二つ）。一の字は、上が定規の直線、下が大きな弧です。左の端は下の円と同じ大きさの円で、上を斜めの直線で切ります。右の端は少し小さい円で、下に斜めの直線を引きます。上の線は、両端でとがって上がります。

12 guides, 4 shapes, 28 steps.

## Well frame · 井桁 (`igeta`)

Four bars laid as a square stood on its corner, like the character 井 turned an eighth of a turn: two lean each way half a right angle, and their ends stand out past the crossings by about a bar's width.

四本の棒を、井の字を45度回したように、角で立つ正方形に組みます。二本ずつ左右に45度傾け、棒の端は、交わるところから棒の太さほど外に出します。

13 guides, 4 shapes, 24 steps.

## Left three-comma tomoe · 左三つ巴 (`hidari-mitsu-tomoe`)

On each of three spokes a third of a turn apart stand two circles that touch the outer circle from inside at the spoke's end: the head, 0.45 of the radius, and the swirl, 0.66. A comma is three arcs: the outer circle from the end of the spoke behind to its own, its head, and the swirl of the spoke behind. The white is three horns, each the crescent between a swirl and its head, and each tail thins to a point on the outer circle, which stays whole.

120度ずつ離れた三本の線の上に、線の端で外円に内側から接する円を二つずつ置きます。頭の円は半径の0.45、渦の円は0.66です。巴一つは三つの弧でできます。後ろの線の端から自分の線の端までの外円、頭の円、そして後ろの線の渦の円です。白い部分は三本の角の形で、それぞれ渦の円と頭の円の間の三日月です。尾は外円の上で細くなって尖り、外円は欠けません。

6 guides, 3 shapes, 14 steps.

## Sixteen-petal chrysanthemum · 十六八重表菊 (`juroku-yae-giku`)

The sixteen-division: sixteen petals packed edge to edge round a small centre disc, each petal's two sides parallel to spokes a sixteenth of a turn apart with a thin white line between neighbours, and each ending in a round tip, the circle that touches both sides; at the rim, between the front tips, show the round tips of sixteen petals behind, turned half a petal.

十六割で割り付けます。中心の小さな円のまわりに花弁を16枚すき間なく並べ、各花弁の両側を22.5度ずつ離した放射線に平行にとって隣との間に細い白線を残し、先は両側に接する円で丸く収めます。外周では表の花弁の先の間から、半枚ずらした後ろの16枚の丸い先をのぞかせます。

18 guides, 33 shapes, 35 steps.

## Wood sorrel · 片喰 (`katabami`)

Three broad heart leaves that nearly touch, their points at the centre. A leaf is two circles side by side and the two ruled lines from the centre that touch them. Between the leaves stand three short stamens with round ends, and a small disc sits in a white ring at the centre.

幅の広い心形の葉を三枚、葉先を中心に向けて、ほとんど接するように置きます。葉は、並んだ二つの円と、中心からその円に接する二本の直線でできます。葉の間に、先の丸い短いしべを三本立て、中心には白い輪の中に小さな円を置きます。

15 guides, 4 shapes, 28 steps.

## Bellflower · 桔梗 (`kikyo`)

Five petals. On each side of a petal a round shoulder, the arc of a circle from the notch between petals, and touching it a flatter arc that runs up to the tip, where the two sides meet in a small sharp point; below the notches its sides are ruled to the centre. A white gap parts neighbours, a short white ray runs up the middle of each petal, and in the centre is a plain round disc with a white line round it.

花弁は五枚です。花弁の両側は、花弁の間の切れ込みから始まる円の弧（丸い肩）と、それに接して先まで伸びるゆるい弧でできていて、左右の弧が先で小さく尖って出会います。切れ込みから下の辺は、中心への直線です。隣どうしは白い隙間で分け、各花弁の中央に短い白い線を一本入れます。中心には、白い線で囲んだ丸を置きます。

19 guides, 6 shapes, 33 steps.

## Three oak leaves · 三つ柏 (`mitsu-gashiwa`)

Three oak leaves a third of a turn apart, their stalks at the centre. A leaf's smooth edge is five circles: one at the top, a shoulder each side and a foot each side. A white midrib runs up it, and three white veins each side leave the midrib low, bow out beside the edge and run up towards the top, each an arc. A small disc sits in a white ring where the leaves meet.

柏の葉を三枚、葉柄を中心に集めて120度ずつ離して置きます。葉のなめらかな縁は五つの円（上に一つ、左右の肩に一つずつ、左右の下に一つずつ）でできます。白い中肋を一本通し、左右三本ずつの白い葉脈を、中肋の下の方から縁に沿ってふくらませ、上へ向かわせます。葉脈はどれも弧です。葉が集まるところに、白い輪の中の小さな円を置きます。

## Three ginkgo leaves · 三つ銀杏 (`mitsu-icho`)

Three fan-shaped leaves a third of a turn apart round a hub, filling a circle. Thirty equal small circles on one circle make the wavy edge. Between the leaves, three white lenses run from the hub to the edge, each two equal arcs. Each leaf has a ruled middle vein, an arc vein each side of it, and two small curls where its edge rolls into a loop.

## Three hollyhock leaves · 三つ葉葵 (`mitsuba-aoi`)

Three heart-shaped leaves a third of a turn apart, each with its point at the centre and its two round lobes outside, white stems between them. Every edge is an arc of a compass circle or a ruled line: each lobe is one circle, its straight side a line through two grid points, and its veins arcs and lines 1.7 wide that fork towards shared points on the edge. Between the two lobes a black spindle runs up the leaf's axis from near the centre and ends in a point at the notch; beside it the outline rolls into a curl with a hook.

心形の葉を三枚、120度ずつ離し、葉先を中心に、二つの丸い葉の耳を外側に向けて置き、葉の間を白い茎で分けます。すべての縁はコンパスの円の弧か、定規の直線です。葉の耳は一つの円、まっすぐな辺は二つの格子点を通る直線、葉脈は幅1.7の弧と直線で、縁の同じ点に向かって枝分かれします。二つの耳の間には、中心近くから葉の軸に沿って黒い紡錘形が伸び、切れ込みで尖って終わります。その横で、葉の輪郭が鉤のついた渦に巻き込みます。

52 guides, 9 shapes, 85 steps.

## Paulownia, five-three-five · 五三桐 (`gosan-no-kiri`)

Three broad leaves below, the middle one hanging and the two beside it spread, each with its midrib and veins; above them three upright spikes of florets, five in the middle one and three in each of the others. A leaf is a rounded heart: its two upper edges are one compass width mirrored, and round its body are five scallops of one width. Its veins leave the midrib on both sides and bow toward the tip, each the white between two circles two apart, and stop short of the edge. A floret is a pointed bell of three petals fanned from its base, each petal the pointed lens of two circles of one width; the florets stand in pairs leaning out from the stalk, one on its top, and none touches another. The side leaves are the same plan at four fifths, turned out on a 5-12-13 triangle, and pass under the middle leaf. The left half is struck first and the right half is struck in place as its mirror.

下に広い葉を三枚、中央の葉は垂らし左右の葉は広げて、それぞれ葉脈を入れます。その上に花穂を三本立て、中央に五つ、左右に三つずつ花をつけます。葉は丸みのあるハート形です。上の左右の縁は同じ幅の円で、まわりに同じ幅の丸い裂片を五つ並べます。葉脈は主脈から左右に出て葉先のほうへ弓なりに曲がり、二つの円の間の白いすき間で表し、縁の手前で止めます。花は先のとがった釣鐘形で、根元から三枚の花びらを広げ、花びらは同じ幅の二つの円の重なりで作ります。花は茎の左右に二つずつ外へ傾けて並べ、先に一つ置き、花どうしは離します。左右の葉は同じ形を五分の四の大きさにして外へ回し、中央の葉の下に入れます。左半分を先に作図し、右半分はその場で左右対称に作図します。

Drawn from 伊呂波分新撰紋帳 (Iroha-wake shinsen monchō (a new crest book in syllabary order), ed. Inoue Katsugorō, Kunshidō, 1892), [NDL 780550](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/780550), frame 38, left page, third row, first column: 丸に五三きり, its ring left out (左頁、三段目の一列目「丸に五三きり」（輪は除く）); public domain (保護期間満了); read 2026-11-24.

## Front-facing rabbit · 真向き兎 (`usagi`)

A rabbit crouched and seen from the front. Its body is two equal circles side by side, which cross on the axis and leave a notch under the chin, with two ruled lines from the point of the back touching them. Each ear is the lens between two equal arcs, leaning out. The face is a circle drawn as a white line, with ringed eyes, the line of the nose, four ruled whiskers a side, and an arm coming round to a three-clawed paw.
