# Provenance: how each crest is constructed

Every drawing is made for this package and is MIT. The geometric crests are struck from their construction with compass and ruler.
The organic crests whose source is given below are vectorised from a public-domain period crest book: the page was read at the
National Diet Library, the crest's outline drawing was filled, traced to curves, cleaned of print noise, and made exactly symmetric
by keeping one half (or one leaf) and mirroring or turning it; no scan, crop or picture of the book is in the package or the
repository. This is the one-line rule each crest is set out by, in English and in Japanese, and the page it was drawn from.
Written by `pnpm data`; do not edit it by hand.

## Rings

The weight of a ring and the white inside it are measured from fifteen ringed crests drawn in the usual way (fourteen in a sheet of
ringed crests and one ringed chrysanthemum, the reference pictures the package was checked against): the ring is 0.079 to 0.095 of
the crest's diameter, 0.087 at the median; the white between ring and motif is 0.011 to 0.029 of the diameter, 0.018 at the median;
the motif reaches 0.77 to 0.80 of the outer radius. So a medium ring is 16.8 on the 192 of the layout circle, the white inside it 3.5,
and a ringed motif is drawn to the ring's inside less that white. A thin ring is a thirtieth of the diameter.

## Three hollyhock leaves · 三つ葉葵 (`mitsuba-aoi`)

Three heart-shaped leaves a third of a turn apart, each with its point at the centre and its two round lobes outside, white stems between them. Every edge is an arc of a compass circle or a ruled line: each lobe is one circle, its straight side a line through two grid points, and its veins arcs and lines 1.7 wide that fork towards shared points on the edge. Between the two lobes a black spindle runs up the leaf's axis from near the centre and ends in a point at the notch; beside it the outline rolls into a curl with a hook.

心形の葉を三枚、120度ずつ離し、葉先を中心に、二つの丸い葉の耳を外側に向けて置き、葉の間を白い茎で分けます。すべての縁はコンパスの円の弧か、定規の直線です。葉の耳は一つの円、まっすぐな辺は二つの格子点を通る直線、葉脈は幅1.7の弧と直線で、縁の同じ点に向かって枝分かれします。二つの耳の間には、中心近くから葉の軸に沿って黒い紡錘形が伸び、切れ込みで尖って終わります。その横で、葉の輪郭が鉤のついた渦に巻き込みます。

52 guides, 9 shapes, 85 steps.

## Three hollyhock leaves in a circle · 丸に三つ葉葵 (`maru-ni-mitsuba-aoi`)

The three hollyhock leaves inside a ring about a tenth of the diameter thick (100 and 81 about the centre). Each leaf's black spindle widens above the notch into a wedge that joins the ring, with a white horn either side of it between the lobe and the ring.

三つ葉葵を、直径の約10分の1の太さの輪（中心から100と81の円）の中に収めます。各葉の黒い紡錘形は、切れ込みの上でくさび形に広がって輪につながり、その両側、葉の耳と輪の間に白い角の形が残ります。

56 guides, 10 shapes, 93 steps.

## Standing hollyhock · 立ち葵 (`tachi-aoi`)

Three hollyhock leaves on stems that rise from one foot: one leaf upright at the top and one a quarter turn to each side, their tips on the layout circle and their notches towards the centre. The leaf is the three-leaf hollyhock's, with its white veins and its curls. A stem 6 wide comes in between a leaf's two lobes and runs on as the black spindle. The top leaf's stem goes straight down to the base. The left branch leaves its leaf between two arcs and joins the upright stem; the right branch passes behind it, so a white line 3 wide parts the two all the way down. Below the branches the trunk flares into a foot that stands on a level line, each side an arc of a circle 100 wide. The top leaf is built first, then the right leaf and the left leaf where they stand, clockwise, and the stems last.

葵の葉を三枚、一つの根元から立ち上がる茎につけます。一枚は上にまっすぐ立て、残りの二枚は左右に90度ずつ回して置きます。葉先は三枚とも外の円の上にあり、切れ込みは中心を向きます。葉は三つ葉葵と同じで、白い葉脈と渦があります。幅6の茎が二つの耳の間から入り、そのまま黒い紡錘形につながります。上の葉の茎は、根元までまっすぐ下ります。左の枝は、二つの弧にはさまれて葉から出て、まっすぐな茎に合わさります。右の枝は、まっすぐな茎の後ろを通るので、二つの間に幅3の白い線が根元まで残ります。枝の下で幹は左右に広がり、水平な線の上に立つ根元になります。広がりの両側は、幅100の円の弧です。上の葉を作図し、右の葉、左の葉の順に、時計回りにその場で作図して、最後に茎を作図します。

Measured: The 立あふひ of the 1892 crest book (frame 36, left page, row 3, column 3) and a Wikimedia Commons picture of 立ち葵 (Japanese crest Tachi Aoi.svg, public domain), measured as numbers only, in the unit of the hollyhock leaf (the leaf is 99.4 wide and 68.4 long). In the Commons picture the top leaf's tip stands 117 from the point the three leaves turn about and the side leaves' tips 114; the stems are 6 wide; a white line 3 wide parts the upright stem from the right branch; the trunk is 26 wide at its narrowest, and the foot is 92 wide on a base 96 below that point. The book draws the same arrangement by hand and in outline, with the side leaves tilted upwards, rounder leaves with a small curl each side of the stem, and the same white line to the right of the upright stem. Ours keeps the package's hollyhock leaf (straight sides, and large round shoulders at the notch where both pictures have small curls), sets the side leaves level as the Commons picture does, and makes them the top leaf a quarter turn about the centre, 115 out.

Measured from 伊呂波分新撰紋帳 (Iroha-wake shinsen monchō (a new crest book in syllabary order), ed. Inoue Katsugorō, Kunshidō, 1892), [NDL 780550](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/780550), frame 36, left page, third row, third column: 立あふひ (measured, and drawn with compass and ruler) (左頁、三段目の三列目「立あふひ」（寸法を測り、コンパスと定規で描いたもの）); public domain (保護期間満了); read 2026-11-24.

65 guides, 11 shapes, 108 steps.

## Standing hollyhock in a circle · 丸に立ち葵 (`maru-ni-tachi-aoi`)

The standing hollyhock inside a ring about a tenth of the diameter thick. The leaves stand closer to the centre than in the crest on its own and the branches are shorter, so that the three leaves fill the ring. The white line between the upright stem and the right branch is 4 wide. The left branch's upper edge touches the upright stem, so the white between the two closes to a point.

立ち葵を、直径の約10分の1の太さの輪の中に収めます。輪のない立ち葵より葉を中心に寄せ、枝を短くして、三枚の葉で輪の中を埋めます。まっすぐな茎と右の枝の間の白い線は、幅4です。左の枝の上の縁は、まっすぐな茎に接します。そのため、二つの間の白は一点で閉じます。

Measured: The 丸に左りはなれ葵 of the 1892 crest book (frame 36, right page, row 4, column 4) and a Wikimedia Commons picture of the Honda house's crest (Japanese crest Honda Tachi Aoi.svg, public domain), measured as numbers only, in the unit of the hollyhock leaf. In the Commons picture the ring is 0.086 of the diameter thick; the tips stand 104 from the centre, 0.75 of the radius; the foot's base is 92 below the centre; the stems are 7 wide and the white line beside the upright stem 4; the left branch's upper edge runs into the upright stem without a corner. Both pictures draw the leaves larger for the ring and closer to the centre than in the crest on its own. The book names its cell a hollyhock in a circle with the left one apart and draws a thin double ring; the Commons picture is the same drawing in a thick ring, named the Honda standing hollyhock. Ours has the ring of the package's ringed crests, and its leaves reach 0.79 of the radius.

## Three ginkgo leaves · 三つ銀杏 (`mitsu-icho`)

Three fan-shaped leaves a third of a turn apart round a hub, filling a circle. Thirty equal small circles on one circle make the wavy edge. Between the leaves, three white lenses run from the hub to the edge, each two equal arcs. Each leaf has a ruled middle vein, an arc vein each side of it, and two small curls where its edge rolls into a loop.

扇形の葉を三枚、芯の円のまわりに120度ずつ離して置き、全体を円に収めます。一つの円周上に同じ大きさの小円を三十並べて、波打つ縁を作ります。葉の間には、同じ半径の二つの弧でできた白いレンズ形を、芯から縁まで三つ通します。各葉には、定規で引いた中央の葉脈、その左右に弧の葉脈、縁が小さな輪に巻き込む渦を二つ入れます。

## Standing water plantain · 立ち沢瀉 (`omodaka`)

A leaf of three pointed lobes of one size about a small ring at the centre: one upright, and one down to each side, 45 degrees from straight down. Each lobe is the lens between two arcs of one radius, with a white vein from the ring towards its tip. The upright lobe is built in full, and the other two are built the same way where they stand. A spray of five three-petalled flowers stands each side of the leaf, and three stems hang under it: the middle one straight and thickest, the outer two thin and bowed outward.

真ん中の小さな輪から、先のとがった同じ大きさの裂片を三つ出します。一つはまっすぐ上、あとの二つは左右の下向きで、真下から45度ずつ開きます。裂片はどれも、同じ半径の二つの弧にはさまれた形で、輪から裂片の先へ白い葉脈を一本通します。上の裂片を先に最後まで作図し、あとの二つは、同じ作図をそれぞれ立つ場所でそのまま行います。葉の左右に、花びら三枚の花を五つ付けた枝を一本ずつ立てます。葉の下には茎を三本垂らします。真ん中の茎はまっすぐで一番太く、外の二本は細く、外へ弓なりに曲がります。

Measured: The standing water plantain of the 1892 crest book (frame 14, 立ちおもだか), with two present-day drawings of it and of the ringed crest for comparison, in units of a lobe's length from the centre ring to its tip. The three lobes are one length in all three. The upright lobe is 0.55 to 0.58 as wide as it is long, widest a little over a third of the way up, and each edge is close to one arc of 0.87. The lower lobes stand 49 to 53 degrees from straight down in the book and 41 to 45 in the present-day drawings; here they are 45. The ring at the centre is 0.07 across, the white round it and the white veins about 0.02 wide. The middle stem is 0.06 wide, the outer two 0.04 and bowed outward. In the book and in the present-day drawings the lower lobes bend outward a little and are fuller above the vein than below it; here each is the upright lobe turned.

Drawn from 伊呂波分新撰紋帳 (Iroha-wake shinsen monchō (a new crest book in syllabary order), ed. Inoue Katsugorō, Kunshidō, 1892), [NDL 780550](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/780550), frame 14, right page, second row, third column: 立ちおもだか (measured, and drawn afresh with compass and ruler) (右頁、二段目の三列目「立ちおもだか」（寸法を測り、コンパスと定規で描き直したもの）); public domain (保護期間満了); read 2026-11-24.

## Maple leaf · 紅葉 (`momiji`)

Seven pointed lobes about a small ring low on the leaf, each the lens between two arcs of one radius: the top one the longest, and each pair shorter down to the lowest two, with deep, narrow gaps between them. A straight white vein runs from the ring towards every tip. A short stem hangs under the ring, a little wider at its flat end.

葉の下の方にある小さな輪のまわりに、先のとがった七つの裂片を広げます。裂片はどれも、同じ半径の二つの弧にはさまれた形です。一番上が一番長く、左右の組は下へいくほど短くなり、裂片の間は深く細く切れこみます。輪からそれぞれの裂片の先へ、まっすぐな白い葉脈を一本ずつ通します。輪の下に短い茎を垂らし、平らな切り口の方を少し太くします。

Measured: The leaf's proportions are measured, as numbers only, from a present-day drawing of 一つ楓; no drawing or outline from it is included. In units of the top lobe's length from the small ring where the veins meet: the lobes stand 0, 48, 93 and 132 degrees from upright and are 1, 0.85, 0.61 and 0.45 long, each about a third as wide as it is long and fuller on its upper side; the gaps between them reach in to 0.44, 0.35 and 0.33 from the ring, and the lowest two lobes meet 0.16 under it. The ring is 0.07 across with a white line round it, and the stem hangs to 0.4 under it, widening to its flat end. Ours is within 0.02 of each of these, every lobe the lens between two circles of 87; its veins run straight from the ring to every tip, where the drawing's bow upward, by up to 0.09 in the upper lobes. The crest's period source is the 1892 crest book (frame 44, 紅葉), measured the same way for comparison: its lobes stand 0, 41, 74 and 123 degrees from upright and are 1, 0.89, 0.75 and 0.55 long, and it has a white arc across the foot, veins that start from that arc, and a broad stem.

Drawn from 伊呂波分新撰紋帳 (Iroha-wake shinsen monchō (a new crest book in syllabary order), ed. Inoue Katsugorō, Kunshidō, 1892), [NDL 780550](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/780550), frame 44, left page, third row, first column: 紅葉 (measured, and drawn afresh with compass and ruler) (左頁、三段目の一列目「紅葉」（寸法を測り、コンパスと定規で描き直したもの）); public domain (保護期間満了); read 2026-11-24.

## Paper-mulberry leaf · 梶の葉 (`kaji-no-ha`)

A leaf of five round lobes on a short stem: one at the top, one to each side and two below, standing on a round middle that the bays between them reach in to. A lobe's edge is nine scallops, circles two fifths of the lobe wide on the grid points nearest a ring about its middle. A white rib runs along each lobe from the point on the axis where the ribs meet, with white veins leaving it on both sides: four a side in the top lobe and three a side in the others. Every rib and vein ends on a circle a little inside its lobe's edge. The stem lies over the leaf with a white line round it. The right half is built in full, then the stem, then the left half where it stands.

短い柄のついた、丸い裂片が五つある葉です。裂片は上に一つ、左右に一つずつ、下に二つあり、まん中の丸い部分につながります。裂片の間の切れ込みは、その円までです。裂片の縁は、小さな円九つでできた波の形です。中心線の上の一点から、それぞれの裂片へ白い葉脈を一本引き、その両側に白い葉脈を出します。上の裂片は四本ずつ、ほかの裂片は三本ずつです。白い葉脈はどれも、裂片の縁より少し内側の円で終わります。柄は葉の上に重ね、まわりに白い線を残します。右半分、柄の順に作図し、左半分はその場で作図します。

Measured: The 梶の葉 of the 1892 crest book (frame 17, left page, row 2, column 2), measured as numbers only, in units of half the leaf's height with its stem. Five round lobes: the top one 0.8 across and 0.8 tall, its top at 0.97; one each side 0.7 across that reaches 0.97 from the axis, its middle a little over the centre; and two below, 0.67 across, down to 0.88 under the centre. The bays between the lobes reach in to about 0.5 from a point 0.12 under the centre. The veins meet on the axis 0.5 under the centre; a midrib runs up the top lobe with five or six veins a side, four or five veins fan into each side lobe, and each low lobe has a level rib with four veins hanging from it. The stem is 0.14 wide and ends 0.98 under the centre. The book draws it in outline with wavy edges, curved veins and a small fold where two lobes meet; ours is solid, as a crest is worn, the left half the mirror of the right, each lobe's edge nine round scallops, with a straight white rib along every lobe from the point where they meet, four straight white veins a side in the top lobe and three a side in the others, as the usual drawing of this crest has them (a Wikimedia Commons picture, Kaji-no-Ha, was looked at for comparison only).

Measured from 伊呂波分新撰紋帳 (Iroha-wake shinsen monchō (a new crest book in syllabary order), ed. Inoue Katsugorō, Kunshidō, 1892), [NDL 780550](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/780550), frame 17, left page, second row, second column: 梶の葉 (measured, and drawn with compass and ruler) (左頁、二段目の二列目「梶の葉」（寸法を測り、コンパスと定規で描いたもの）); public domain (保護期間満了); read 2026-11-25.

## Three oak leaves · 三つ柏 (`mitsu-gashiwa`)

Three oak leaves a third of a turn apart, their stalks at the centre. A leaf's smooth edge is five circles: one at the top, a shoulder each side and a foot each side. A white midrib runs up it, and three white veins each side leave the midrib low, bow out beside the edge and run up towards the top, each an arc. A small disc sits in a white ring where the leaves meet.
