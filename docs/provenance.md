# Provenance: how each crest is constructed

Every drawing is made for this package and is MIT. The geometric crests are struck from their construction with compass and ruler.
The organic crests whose source is given below are vectorised from a public-domain period crest book: the page was read at the
National Diet Library, the crest's outline drawing was filled, traced to curves, cleaned of print noise, and made exactly symmetric
by keeping one half (or one leaf) and mirroring or turning it; no scan, crop or picture of the book is in the package or the
repository. This is the one-line rule each crest is set out by, in English and in Japanese, and the page it was drawn from.
Written by `pnpm data`; do not edit it by hand.

## Rings

The weight of a ring and the white inside it are measured from fifteen ringed crests drawn in the usual way (fourteen in a sheet of
ringed crests and one ringed chrysanthemum, the reference pictures the package was checked against): the ring is 0.079 to 0.095 of
the crest's diameter, 0.087 at the median; the white between ring and motif is 0.011 to 0.029 of the diameter, 0.018 at the median;
the motif reaches 0.77 to 0.80 of the outer radius. So a medium ring is 16.8 on the 192 of the layout circle, the white inside it 3.5,
and a ringed motif is drawn to the ring's inside less that white. A thin ring is a thirtieth of the diameter.

## Three hollyhock leaves · 三つ葉葵 (`mitsuba-aoi`)

Three heart-shaped leaves a third of a turn apart, each with its point at the centre and its two round lobes outside, white stems between them. Every edge is an arc of a compass circle or a ruled line: each lobe is one circle, its straight side a line through two grid points, and its veins arcs and lines 1.7 wide that fork towards shared points on the edge. Between the two lobes a black spindle runs up the leaf's axis from near the centre and ends in a point at the notch; beside it the outline rolls into a curl with a hook.

心形の葉を三枚、120度ずつ離し、葉先を中心に、二つの丸い葉の耳を外側に向けて置き、葉の間を白い茎で分けます。すべての縁はコンパスの円の弧か、定規の直線です。葉の耳は一つの円、まっすぐな辺は二つの格子点を通る直線、葉脈は幅1.7の弧と直線で、縁の同じ点に向かって枝分かれします。二つの耳の間には、中心近くから葉の軸に沿って黒い紡錘形が伸び、切れ込みで尖って終わります。その横で、葉の輪郭が鉤のついた渦に巻き込みます。

52 guides, 9 shapes, 85 steps.

## Three ginkgo leaves · 三つ銀杏 (`mitsu-icho`)

Three fan-shaped leaves a third of a turn apart round a hub, filling a circle. Thirty equal small circles on one circle make the wavy edge. Between the leaves, three white lenses run from the hub to the edge, each two equal arcs. Each leaf has a ruled middle vein, an arc vein each side of it, and two small curls where its edge rolls into a loop.

扇形の葉を三枚、芯の円のまわりに120度ずつ離して置き、全体を円に収めます。一つの円周上に同じ大きさの小円を三十並べて、波打つ縁を作ります。葉の間には、同じ半径の二つの弧でできた白いレンズ形を、芯から縁まで三つ通します。各葉には、定規で引いた中央の葉脈、その左右に弧の葉脈、縁が小さな輪に巻き込む渦を二つ入れます。

## Standing water plantain · 立ち沢瀉 (`omodaka`)

A leaf of three pointed lobes of one size about a small ring at the centre: one upright, and one down to each side, 45 degrees from straight down. Each lobe is the lens between two arcs of one radius, with a white vein from the ring towards its tip. The upright lobe is built in full, and the other two are built the same way where they stand. A spray of five three-petalled flowers stands each side of the leaf, and three stems hang under it: the middle one straight and thickest, the outer two thin and bowed outward.

真ん中の小さな輪から、先のとがった同じ大きさの裂片を三つ出します。一つはまっすぐ上、あとの二つは左右の下向きで、真下から45度ずつ開きます。裂片はどれも、同じ半径の二つの弧にはさまれた形で、輪から裂片の先へ白い葉脈を一本通します。上の裂片を先に最後まで作図し、あとの二つは、同じ作図をそれぞれ立つ場所でそのまま行います。葉の左右に、花びら三枚の花を五つ付けた枝を一本ずつ立てます。葉の下には茎を三本垂らします。真ん中の茎はまっすぐで一番太く、外の二本は細く、外へ弓なりに曲がります。

Measured: The standing water plantain of the 1892 crest book (frame 14, 立ちおもだか), with two present-day drawings of it and of the ringed crest for comparison, in units of a lobe's length from the centre ring to its tip. The three lobes are one length in all three. The upright lobe is 0.55 to 0.58 as wide as it is long, widest a little over a third of the way up, and each edge is close to one arc of 0.87. The lower lobes stand 49 to 53 degrees from straight down in the book and 41 to 45 in the present-day drawings; here they are 45. The ring at the centre is 0.07 across, the white round it and the white veins about 0.02 wide. The middle stem is 0.06 wide, the outer two 0.04 and bowed outward. In the book and in the present-day drawings the lower lobes bend outward a little and are fuller above the vein than below it; here each is the upright lobe turned.

Drawn from 伊呂波分新撰紋帳 (Iroha-wake shinsen monchō (a new crest book in syllabary order), ed. Inoue Katsugorō, Kunshidō, 1892), [NDL 780550](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/780550), frame 14, right page, second row, third column: 立ちおもだか (measured, and drawn afresh with compass and ruler) (右頁、二段目の三列目「立ちおもだか」（寸法を測り、コンパスと定規で描き直したもの）); public domain (保護期間満了); read 2026-11-24.

## Maple leaf · 紅葉 (`momiji`)

Seven pointed lobes about a small ring low on the leaf, each the lens between two arcs of one radius: the top one the longest, and each pair shorter down to the lowest two, with deep, narrow gaps between them. A straight white vein runs from the ring towards every tip. A short stem hangs under the ring, a little wider at its flat end.

葉の下の方にある小さな輪のまわりに、先のとがった七つの裂片を広げます。裂片はどれも、同じ半径の二つの弧にはさまれた形です。一番上が一番長く、左右の組は下へいくほど短くなり、裂片の間は深く細く切れこみます。輪からそれぞれの裂片の先へ、まっすぐな白い葉脈を一本ずつ通します。輪の下に短い茎を垂らし、平らな切り口の方を少し太くします。

Measured: The leaf's proportions are measured, as numbers only, from a present-day drawing of 一つ楓; no drawing or outline from it is included. In units of the top lobe's length from the small ring where the veins meet: the lobes stand 0, 48, 93 and 132 degrees from upright and are 1, 0.85, 0.61 and 0.45 long, each about a third as wide as it is long and fuller on its upper side; the gaps between them reach in to 0.44, 0.35 and 0.33 from the ring, and the lowest two lobes meet 0.16 under it. The ring is 0.07 across with a white line round it, and the stem hangs to 0.4 under it, widening to its flat end. Ours is within 0.02 of each of these, every lobe the lens between two circles of 87; its veins run straight from the ring to every tip, where the drawing's bow upward, by up to 0.09 in the upper lobes. The crest's period source is the 1892 crest book (frame 44, 紅葉), measured the same way for comparison: its lobes stand 0, 41, 74 and 123 degrees from upright and are 1, 0.89, 0.75 and 0.55 long, and it has a white arc across the foot, veins that start from that arc, and a broad stem.

Drawn from 伊呂波分新撰紋帳 (Iroha-wake shinsen monchō (a new crest book in syllabary order), ed. Inoue Katsugorō, Kunshidō, 1892), [NDL 780550](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/780550), frame 44, left page, third row, first column: 紅葉 (measured, and drawn afresh with compass and ruler) (左頁、三段目の一列目「紅葉」（寸法を測り、コンパスと定規で描き直したもの）); public domain (保護期間満了); read 2026-11-24.

## Three oak leaves · 三つ柏 (`mitsu-gashiwa`)

## Wood sorrel · 片喰 (`katabami`)

Three heart leaves, their points at the centre. A leaf's two round lobes are two circles; its sides are ruled from the centre to touch them. Under the lobes a small white sepal runs down to a plain disc in a white ring at the centre. The leaf on top is built in full, and the other two are built the same way where they stand, clockwise.

心形の葉を三枚、葉先を中心に向けて置きます。葉の丸い裂片は並んだ二つの円で、両脇は中心からその円に接する直線です。裂片の下に、小さな白いがくを、中心の白い輪まで入れます。輪の中は無地の円です。上の葉を先に作図し、残りの二枚は、同じ作り方でその場で時計回りに作図します。

Ours has lobes of 29 about (±24, 52), the same 34, 66 and 25 in a reach of 100, and a disc of 14 in a white ring to 16, so 19. The book's three sepals stand on the leaves' axes, a short stalk widening to a point, as ours do.

## Bellflower · 桔梗 (`kikyo`)

Five petals. On each side of a petal a round shoulder, the arc of a circle from the notch between petals, and touching it a flatter arc that runs up to the tip, where the two sides meet in a small sharp point; below the notches its sides are ruled to the centre. A white gap parts neighbours, a short white ray runs up the middle of each petal, and in the centre is a plain round disc with a white line round it.

花弁は五枚です。花弁の両側は、花弁の間の切れ込みから始まる円の弧（丸い肩）と、それに接して先まで伸びるゆるい弧でできていて、左右の弧が先で小さく尖って出会います。切れ込みから下の辺は、中心への直線です。隣どうしは白い隙間で分け、各花弁の中央に短い白い線を一本入れます。中心には、白い線で囲んだ丸を置きます。

## Sixteen-petal chrysanthemum · 十六八重表菊 (`juroku-yae-giku`)

The sixteen-division: sixteen petals packed edge to edge round a small centre disc, each petal's two sides parallel to spokes a sixteenth of a turn apart with a thin white line between neighbours, and each ending in a round tip, the circle that touches both sides; at the rim, between the front tips, show the round tips of sixteen petals behind, turned half a petal.

十六割で割り付けます。中心の小さな円のまわりに花弁を16枚すき間なく並べ、各花弁の両側を22.5度ずつ離した放射線に平行にとって隣との間に細い白線を残し、先は両側に接する円で丸く収めます。外周では表の花弁の先の間から、半枚ずらした後ろの16枚の丸い先をのぞかせます。

## Paulownia, five-three-five · 五三桐 (`gosan-no-kiri`)

Three broad leaves below, the middle one hanging and the two beside it spread, each with its midrib and veins; above them three upright spikes of florets, five in the middle one and three in each of the others, each spike packed into a jagged cone with no stalk showing. A leaf is a rounded heart: its two upper edges are one compass width mirrored, and round its body are five scallops of one width. Its veins leave the midrib on both sides and bow toward the tip, each the white between two circles two apart, and stop short of the edge. A floret is a disc beside the stem, its outer edge bitten twice by a small circle into three teeth. A white hook three wide runs over its top and down beside the stem: the white between the floret's circle and one three wider, then between two ruled lines, stopping on a circle struck on the stem, so that the floret hangs from the stem by its foot. Each floret lies over the one above it. The top floret is a disc on the stem, its top bitten four times into five points. The side leaves are the same plan at four fifths, turned out on a 5-12-13 triangle, and pass under the middle leaf. The left half is struck first and the right half is struck in place as its mirror.

下に広い葉を三枚、中央の葉は垂らし左右の葉は広げて、それぞれ葉脈を入れます。その上に花穂を三本立て、中央に五つ、左右に三つずつ花をつけます。花はすき間なく重ねて、ぎざぎざの円すい形にまとめ、茎は外に見せません。葉は丸みのあるハート形です。上の左右の縁は同じ幅の円で、まわりに同じ幅の丸い裂片を五つ並べます。葉脈は主脈から左右に出て葉先のほうへ弓なりに曲がり、二つの円の間の白いすき間で表し、縁の手前で止めます。花は茎の横に置いた円で、外側の縁を小さな円で二か所切り取り、三つの歯にします。花の上から茎の横へ、幅3の白い線を回します。白い線は、花の円とそれより3大きい円の間のすき間で、茎の横では二本の直線の間を通り、茎の上に描いた円のところで止まります。こうすると、花は根元だけで茎につながります。下の花は、上の花の手前に重ねます。いちばん上の花は茎の上に置いた円で、上の縁を四か所切り取り、五つの山にします。左右の葉は同じ形を五分の四の大きさにして外へ回し、中央の葉の下に入れます。左半分を先に作図し、右半分はその場で左右対称に作図します。

Measured: Read as parts of the crest's half-width (88 of the layout's 100), upward from the joint. The book's 五七きり: the middle spike a cone reaching 29 from its stem at its foot and 82 high, the side spikes 53 to 54 out and 60 to 66 high; its 丸に五三きり, squeezed into its ring, is taller still (91). The florets' places were read the same way from two solid paulownias, one of five and three florets and one of seven and five (nos. 1110 and 1119; proportions measured, as numbers only, from 日本家紋大図鑑; no drawing or outline from it is included): the middle spike 77 to 79 high, its florets reaching 28, 23 and 21 from the stem (28, 23 and a top floret of 14 where there are five); a side spike 56 to 58 out, 57 to 59 high and reaching 21 at its foot; the white of a hook 3 to 4 and the stem 4. Ours: the middle spike 76 high and reaching 27 at its foot, the side spikes 56 out and 52 high, the hook 3 and the stem 4. Ours stand lower than the books' because the lowest florets rest on our leaves, which hang lower from the joint.

Measured from 伊呂波分新撰紋帳 (Iroha-wake shinsen monchō (a new crest book in syllabary order), ed. Inoue Katsugorō, Kunshidō, 1892), [NDL 780550](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/780550), frame 38, left page, third row, first column: 丸に五三きり, its ring left out (左頁、三段目の一列目「丸に五三きり」（輪は除く）); public domain (保護期間満了); read 2026-11-24.

93 guides, 8 shapes, 120 steps.
