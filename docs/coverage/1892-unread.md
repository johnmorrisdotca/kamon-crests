# Crest names in the 1892 book that were not read

Names in 伊呂波分新撰紋帳 (1892), NDL 780550 (volume 1), that could not be read with confidence, so none was added to the list. Frame is the scan's page number; page is the page of the two-page spread; row counts from the top and column from the right, the way the book is read. Pages transcribed: every crest page of NDL 780550 (frames 5 to 45; frames 46 to 59 are the construction diagrams and ground patterns, with no crest names to list) and, of NDL 780551, only frames 11 and 12. The rest of NDL 780551 (伊呂波分紋帳大全, names printed above each crest, 5 columns by 2 rows a page) is not yet transcribed.
