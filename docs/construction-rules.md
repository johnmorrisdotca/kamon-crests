# Construction rules

Required reading before a crest is drawn, redrawn or reviewed. Each rule is one that can be checked: by the test named beside it
(`pnpm check` runs them all), or, where no test can see it, on the side-by-side sheet. A rule marked "checked on the sheet" is the
reviewer's to hold, crest by crest. The Japanese follows the English.

| # | Rule | How it is checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Compass and ruler only.** A crest is compass circles, arcs taken from circles that were struck, and ruled lines. No Bézier curve, no stroke, no offset path, no traced run of short lines. | `src/compass.test.ts`, "is fills outlined in arcs and ruled lines only". The crests still to be rebuilt are named in its `PENDING` list, and a crest on the list that passes fails the test until it is taken off. |
| 2 | **Whole circles, struck first.** Every guide is a full circle or a ruled line, struck before any arc is taken from it. Every arc of the finished crest lies on a struck circle and every straight edge on a ruled line. | `src/compass.test.ts`, "takes every arc from a circle struck in full, and every straight edge from a ruled line" (within 0.05 of a unit). `src/steps.test.ts`, "has guides, shapes and ordered steps that draw each exactly once": the guides come before the shapes. A ruled line runs from a point already on the page to another (the centre, or a point on a circle or line struck before): `src/compass.test.ts`, "rules every line from a point already on the page to another". Every line now ends on the layout circle; the one crest with lines that end on the square instead (rule 13) is counted in its `LOOSE` list, and a number there may only fall. |
| 3 | **A white line is a gap.** White is the space left between two fills, never a stroke. A white line of width w is bounded by arcs of radius r and r + w. | No stroke: `src/compass.test.ts`, as rule 1. The two radii: checked on the sheet. |
| 4 | **Over and under.** Where one piece crosses over another, the gap in the piece beneath is bounded by circles (or lines) offset from the piece on top, and those are struck first. | Checked on the sheet. |
| 5 | **One unit, then the turn.** A repeated part is built once in full; its other places are a turn step or a mirror step, not drawn again. The turn shows the unit's circles and lines struck at each other place before the unit is inked there. | `src/steps.test.ts`, "turns only a unit already drawn" and "strikes the unit's guides at each other place in the animation, clockwise, before the unit is inked there". `src/isolate.test.ts`, "tags every shape and every guide with its part and its unit". |
| 6 | **Rings are measured.** A ring is 0.087 of the diameter, the white inside it 0.018, and the motif reaches 0.79 of the radius. | The weights are `RING_WEIGHT` and `RING_GAP` in `scripts/construct/builder.ts`, used by `ring()`. That a crest uses them and fills its ring: checked on the sheet. |
| 7 | **The animation's phases: nothing is inked before its own guides are struck.** (1) The compass underdrawing: the circles about the crest's centre first, from the outside in, then the rest width by width, the smallest first, each centre marked with its width's number before its circle is struck. (2) The ruled underdrawing: every line, full length (rule 13), each parallel set together. (3) Trace only what is needed, and only along circles and lines already on the page. (4) Thicken. (5) Fill. (6) Finish. The first unit's circles and lines, and the layout's, are all struck before the first trace. A place a turn builds (rule 12) is struck later: its circles are struck and its lines ruled before its own ink, not before any trace, because each place is finished, clockwise, before the next (rule 11). Each step's caption names its phase, in English and Japanese, and a run of lines says which it is: the lines through the centre, or a set of parallels. | `src/steps.test.ts`, "goes by the six phases: the underdrawing by compass width, its ruled lines, the trace, the thickening, the fill, the finish" (the order, the centres and width numbers, and the captions), "has guides, shapes and ordered steps that draw each exactly once" (the last guide of the underdrawing comes before the first trace), "turns go round clockwise from the unit, one place at a time: its guides struck, then its shapes inked" (a turned place's guides are struck after the place before is done and before its own ink), and "marks the centres with a dot and the compass width's number, each on its own switch, and draws line art with the fill off". `src/compass.test.ts`, "inks only along what is struck before its own ink: every arc on a circle, every straight edge on a ruled line, between two crossings": an arc's circle within 0.15 in centre and radius, an edge's line within 0.05, and no curve that is not an arc. |
| 8 | **Measure, never copy.** Measuring a crest's proportions from any reference is allowed. Copying a modern drawing's outline or pixels is not. A public-domain book may be vectorised only as a step in measuring, never as the finished drawing. | Checked on the sheet and in review, against the crest's `source` and `docs/provenance.md`. Rule 1's test refuses a traced outline once a crest is off the `PENDING` list. |
| 9 | **The side-by-side gate.** Every crest is put beside a real reference at the same size, and passes only when a stranger could not tell it from the real form, before it ships. | Checked on the sheet: one row per crest, every row looked at. |
| 10 | **A craftsman's construction is followed.** Where a craftsman has published how a crest is constructed (Hatoba's UWAEMON, NHK's デザインあ), ours uses the same circles in the same order. | Checked on the sheet, against the published construction, and credited in the crest's provenance. |
| 11 | **Clockwise, from the top.** Every circle is struck as one clockwise sweep from its top (12 o'clock). A part's circles about the crest's centre come first, the outermost first and inwards, since the field is set out before anything inside it (John, 2026-11-25: "it doesn't make sense to draw the inner circles before the outer circle"); then its other circles by compass width, the smallest first, and those of one width clockwise by their centres; then its ruled lines, each parallel set together: the sets clockwise from upright, a set's lines from the top down (upright lines from the left), each line ruled from its upper end. Repeated places are visited clockwise, one at a time: a place's guides, then its ink, before the next. It orders the steps only and never changes the finished drawing. | `src/compass.test.ts`, "strikes every circle clockwise from its top, a part's circles about the centre first from the outside in, then its others by compass width with the smallest first and clockwise by their centres, and then rules its lines, each parallel set together and from the top down". `src/steps.test.ts`, "turns go round clockwise from the unit, one place at a time: its guides struck, then its shapes inked". Not held by a test: that the unit drawn first is the one nearest the top, and that an outline is traced clockwise (it is traced the way its path is written); checked on the sheet. |
| 12 | **Nothing ever moves.** This is about the crest's pieces; the compass arm is the tool, and follows the pen (a thin line from a circle's centre to the pen while the circle is struck or one of its arcs is inked, the centre's dot bold for as long, gone when the curve ends). A mirrored or turned part is never shown as a copy that flips, slides or rotates into place. It is built where it stands, like the first: its own circles struck, its own lines ruled, then its ink, one place at a time. The data may still say that a part is the first unit mirrored or turned (for the counts, a part shown alone and lineage). | `src/steps.test.ts`, "turns go round clockwise from the unit, one place at a time: its guides struck, then its shapes inked": no style of the animation holds a transform but the compass arm's turn about its centre, and each place's guides are struck there before its ink. "has one compass arm for each circle struck and each arc inked, from that curve's centre, gone when the curve ends, and none with the switch off" holds the arm. `src/compass.test.ts`, "takes every arc from a circle struck in full, and every straight edge from a ruled line", holds each shape against the guides struck at its own place. |
| 13 | **Lines are ruled full length.** Nobody draws a square: many long lines are ruled, and the square comes from them. Every ruled line runs from edge to edge of the outer guide circle, never only along the edge it bounds, and the layout circle is struck for it even in a crest with no ring. A bar, a square, a diamond or a lattice is the space between long lines that cross, and the trace inks only the pieces between the crossings. A shape with no arc of its own is never a unit turned: each of its lines is ruled in the underdrawing. Where an inked edge runs past the layout circle (a feather's end in a crest with no ring), its line is ruled from side to side of the square the circle stands in. | `src/compass.test.ts`, "rules every line full length, from edge to edge of the outer guide circle" (both ends within 0.05 of it; the lines that cross the square instead are counted in its `SQUARE` list, and a number there may only fall), and "inks only along what is struck before its own ink: every arc on a circle, every straight edge on a ruled line, between two crossings" (each end of an inked edge stands where another circle or line crosses its line). |
| 14 | **Polygons are never drawn as polygons.** A hexagon, a triangle, a square or a diamond is where full-length lines cross, and its corners are points already on the page. For a hexagon or a triangle: strike the circle; keep the compass at the same width and step it round the circle from 12 o'clock, clockwise, to mark six points; then rule a full-length line through each pair of neighbouring corners. A thin inner outline gets its own lines, parallel to the first and full length too. A square's or a diamond's corners are where the upright and level lines through the centre meet a struck circle. No guide is a polygon's outline. | `src/steps.test.ts`, "marks a hexagon's corners by stepping the compass round a circle, and rules no polygon as a guide" (the six marks from the top, clockwise, each one compass width from the last; the step after its circle and before any line; every guide line a single line), with rule 13's tests. That a square's or a diamond's corners stand on struck circles: checked on the sheet. |
| 15 | **A count is what the animation does.** Like a character's stroke count, a crest's counts are over every place a turn or a mirror builds: `circles` is the circles struck, `lines` the lines ruled, `arcs` the arcs inked, `steps` the player's steps. A guide that lands on one already struck counts once: a circle about the crest's centre, or a line through it that the turn lays on itself, is the same at every place. The compass widths are the distinct widths, however many places use them. | `src/stats.test.ts`, "counts the distinct circles struck, lines ruled and arcs inked in its animation, over every place a turn builds" (each counted again from the animation's own paths) and "counts match its steps". |

## 作図のルール（日本語）

家紋を描く前、描き直す前、確認する前に、かならず読んでください。どのルールも確かめられる形で書いてあります。横にあるテスト
（`pnpm check` がすべて実行します）で確かめるか、テストでは見えないものは、見比べ用のシートで一つずつ確かめます。

| # | ルール | 確かめ方 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **コンパスと定規だけ。** 家紋は、コンパスの円、描いた円から取った弧、定規の直線だけで作ります。ベジェ曲線、線（ストローク）、オフセットしたパス、短い線をつないだなぞり書きは使いません。 | `src/compass.test.ts` の "is fills outlined in arcs and ruled lines only"。まだ描き直していない家紋は `PENDING` に名前があります。 |
| 2 | **円は一周分を先に描く。** 補助線は円一周か定規の直線で、弧を取る前に描きます。仕上がりの弧はすべて描いた円の上に、まっすぐな辺はすべて引いた直線の上にあります。 | `src/compass.test.ts` の "takes every arc from a circle struck in full, and every straight edge from a ruled line"、`src/steps.test.ts` の "has guides, shapes and ordered steps that draw each exactly once"。定規の直線は、すでに紙の上にある点（中心、または先に描いた円や直線の上の点）から、別のそういう点へ引きます：`src/compass.test.ts` の "rules every line from a point already on the page to another"。直線の端は外の円の上にあります。外の四角まで引く直線（ルール13）がある一つの家紋だけ `LOOSE` に数があり、数は減らすことしかできません。 |
| 3 | **白い線はすき間。** 白は二つの塗りの間に残したすき間で、線ではありません。幅 w の白い線は、半径 r と r + w の弧ではさみます。 | 線がないこと：ルール1のテスト。二つの半径：シートで確認。 |
| 4 | **上と下の重なり。** 上を通る部分があるとき、下の部分の切れ目は、上の部分からずらした円（または直線）ではさみ、その円を先に描きます。 | シートで確認。 |
| 5 | **一つ作ってから回す。** くり返す部分は一つだけ最後まで作り、ほかの位置は「回す」か「裏返す」手順で写します。回す手順では、ほかの位置にも同じ円と直線を描いてから、墨を入れます。 | `src/steps.test.ts` の "turns only a unit already drawn" と "strikes the unit's guides at each other place in the animation, clockwise, before the unit is inked there"、`src/isolate.test.ts` の "tags every shape and every guide with its part and its unit"。 |
| 6 | **輪は測った太さ。** 輪は直径の0.087、内側の白は0.018、図柄は半径の0.79まで。 | 太さは `scripts/construct/builder.ts` の `RING_WEIGHT` と `RING_GAP`。使っているかどうかはシートで確認。 |
| 7 | **アニメーションの順番：墨を入れる前に、その場所の補助線を描く。** （1）コンパスの下書き。幅の小さい順で、円を描く前に、中心に幅の番号つきの印をつけます。（2）定規の下書き。直線は全部、端から端まで引きます（ルール13）。平行な線はまとめて引きます。（3）必要な部分だけなぞる。なぞるのは、すでに紙の上にある円と直線の上だけです。（4）太くする。（5）塗る。（6）仕上げ。最初の一つの円と直線、全体の割り付けの円と直線は、最初になぞる前に全部描きます。回して作る位置（ルール12）は別で、その位置の円と直線は「最初になぞる前」ではなく「その位置に墨を入れる前」に描きます。時計回りに一か所ずつ仕上げてから、次の位置へ進むからです（ルール11）。手順の説明に、段階の名前が英語と日本語で出ます。直線の説明は「中心を通る直線」か「平行な直線」のどちらかを示します。 | `src/steps.test.ts` の "goes by the six phases: …"（順番、中心と幅の番号、説明）、"has guides, shapes and ordered steps that draw each exactly once"（下書きの最後の補助線は最初のなぞりより前）、"turns go round clockwise from the unit, one place at a time: its guides struck, then its shapes inked"（回して作る位置の補助線は、前の位置が済んだあと、その位置の墨より前）、"marks the centres with a dot and the compass width's number, each on its own switch, and draws line art with the fill off"。`src/compass.test.ts` の "inks only along what is struck before its own ink: …"（弧は中心と半径が0.15以内の円の上、辺は0.05以内の直線の上、弧でない曲線はなし）。 |
| 8 | **測るのはよい、写すのはだめ。** どの資料からでも比率を測ってかまいません。今の人が描いた図の輪郭や画素を写してはいけません。著作権の切れた本は、測るための途中の作業としてだけベクター化できます。 | シートとレビューで確認（家紋の `source` と `docs/provenance.md`）。 |
| 9 | **見比べの関門。** どの家紋も、本物の資料と同じ大きさで並べ、知らない人が見分けられないときだけ合格です。出す前にかならず行います。 | シートで確認（家紋ごとに一行、全部の行を見る）。 |
| 10 | **職人の作図に合わせる。** 職人が作図を公開している家紋（波戸場承龍さんのUWAEMON、NHK「デザインあ」）は、同じ円を同じ順番で使います。 | 公開された作図と見比べてシートで確認し、出典に書きます。 |
| 11 | **上から時計回り。** 円はどれも、いちばん上（12時）から時計回りに一周で描きます。一つの部分の円は、コンパスの幅の小さい順、同じ幅なら中心が時計回りの順です。そのあと直線を、平行な線ごとにまとめて引きます。まとまりは、縦の線から時計回りの順、まとまりの中は上から下（縦の線は左から）の順で、どの線も上の端から引きます。くり返す位置は時計回りに一か所ずつ回り、その位置の補助線、墨、の順に済ませてから次へ進みます。変わるのは手順だけで、仕上がりの絵は変わりません。 | `src/compass.test.ts` の "strikes every circle clockwise from its top, …"、`src/steps.test.ts` の "turns go round clockwise from the unit, one place at a time: its guides struck, then its shapes inked"。最初に描く一つが上にあること、輪郭を時計回りになぞることは、テストがなく、シートで確認します。 |
| 12 | **何も動かさない。** これは家紋の部品の話です。コンパスの脚は道具なので、ペン先について動きます（円を描く間と弧に墨を入れる間だけ出る、中心からペン先までの細い線。その間は中心の点が太くなり、描き終わると消えます）。裏返した部分や回した部分を、できた形がひっくり返ったり、すべったり、回ったりして入ってくるようには見せません。最初の一つと同じように、その場所で作ります。その場所に円を描き、直線を引き、それから墨を入れ、一か所ずつ進みます。データには「最初の一つを裏返したもの・回したもの」と書いてあってかまいません（数、部品だけの表示、系統のため）。 | `src/steps.test.ts` の "turns go round clockwise from the unit, one place at a time: its guides struck, then its shapes inked"（アニメーションのスタイルで回るのはコンパスの脚だけで、どの位置も補助線が先）と "has one compass arm for each circle struck and each arc inked, …"（コンパスの脚）、`src/compass.test.ts` の "takes every arc from a circle struck in full, and every straight edge from a ruled line"。 |
| 13 | **直線は端から端まで引く。** 四角は描きません。長い線を何本も引くと、その間に四角ができます。直線はどれも、外の円の端から端まで引き、形の辺のところだけを引くことはしません。輪のない家紋でも、そのために外の円を描きます。棒、四角、菱、格子は、交わる長い線の間のすき間で、なぞるのは交点と交点の間だけです。弧のない形は「回して作る」ことをせず、その直線は全部、下書きで引きます。墨の辺が外の円より外へ出る場合（輪のない羽の先）は、円を囲む四角の端から端まで引きます。 | `src/compass.test.ts` の "rules every line full length, from edge to edge of the outer guide circle"（両端が円から0.05以内。四角まで引く線は `SQUARE` に数があり、数は減らすことしかできません）と "inks only along what is struck before its own ink: …"（墨の辺の両端は、ほかの円か直線が交わるところにあります）。 |
| 14 | **多角形は多角形として描かない。** 六角形、三角形、四角形、菱形は、端から端まで引いた直線が交わってできる形で、角はすでに紙の上にある点です。六角形と三角形は、まず円を描き、コンパスを同じ幅のまま、円の上をいちばん上（12時）から時計回りに進めて、六つの点に印をつけます。それから、となり合う角を通る直線を、端から端まで引きます。内側の細い線は、それと平行な直線を別に引きます。四角形と菱形の角は、中心を通る縦と横の線が、描いた円と交わる点です。多角形の輪郭を補助線にすることはありません。 | `src/steps.test.ts` の "marks a hexagon's corners by stepping the compass round a circle, and rules no polygon as a guide"（六つの印がいちばん上から時計回りで、となりとの間がコンパスの幅と同じ。印は円のあと、直線の前。補助の直線はどれも一本の線）と、ルール13のテスト。四角と菱の角が円の上にあることは、シートで確認。 |
| 15 | **数は、アニメーションが実際にすること。** 漢字の画数と同じ考え方です。回したり裏返したりして作る位置もすべて数えます。`circles` は描いた円、`lines` は引いた直線、`arcs` は墨を入れた弧、`steps` はプレーヤーの手順の数です。すでに描いたものと重なる補助線は一つと数えます。家紋の中心に置く円や、回すと元の線に重なる中心を通る直線は、どの位置でも同じものだからです。コンパスの幅は、使う位置がいくつあっても、幅の種類の数です。 | `src/stats.test.ts` の "counts the distinct circles struck, lines ruled and arcs inked in its animation, over every place a turn builds"（アニメーションのパスから数え直します）と "counts match its steps"。 |
