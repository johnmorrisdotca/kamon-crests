# Construction rules

Required reading before a crest is drawn, redrawn or reviewed. Each rule is one that can be checked: by the test named beside it
(`pnpm check` runs them all), or, where no test can see it, on the side-by-side sheet. A rule marked "checked on the sheet" is the
reviewer's to hold, crest by crest. The Japanese follows the English.

| # | Rule | How it is checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Compass and ruler only.** A crest is compass circles, arcs taken from circles that were struck, and ruled lines. No Bézier curve, no stroke, no offset path, no traced run of short lines. | `src/compass.test.ts`, "is fills outlined in arcs and ruled lines only". The crests still to be rebuilt are named in its `PENDING` list, and a crest on the list that passes fails the test until it is taken off. |
| 2 | **Whole circles, struck first.** Every guide is a full circle or a ruled line, struck before any arc is taken from it. Every arc of the finished crest lies on a struck circle and every straight edge on a ruled line. | `src/compass.test.ts`, "takes every arc from a circle struck in full, and every straight edge from a ruled line" (within 0.05 of a unit). `src/steps.test.ts`, "has guides, shapes and ordered steps that draw each exactly once": the guides come before the shapes. A ruled line runs from a point already on the page to another (the centre, or a point on a circle or line struck before): `src/compass.test.ts`, "rules every line from a point already on the page to another". The crests whose lines still stand on nothing are counted in its `LOOSE` list, and a number there may only fall. |
| 3 | **A white line is a gap.** White is the space left between two fills, never a stroke. A white line of width w is bounded by arcs of radius r and r + w. | No stroke: `src/compass.test.ts`, as rule 1. The two radii: checked on the sheet. |
| 4 | **Over and under.** Where one piece crosses over another, the gap in the piece beneath is bounded by circles (or lines) offset from the piece on top, and those are struck first. | Checked on the sheet. |
| 5 | **One unit, then the turn.** A repeated part is built once in full; its other places are a turn step or a mirror step, not drawn again. The turn shows the unit's circles and lines struck at each other place before the unit is inked there. | `src/steps.test.ts`, "turns only a unit already drawn" and "strikes the unit's guides at each other place in the animation". `src/isolate.test.ts`, "tags every shape and every guide with its part and its unit". |
| 6 | **Rings are measured.** A ring is 0.087 of the diameter, the white inside it 0.018, and the motif reaches 0.79 of the radius. | The weights are `RING_WEIGHT` and `RING_GAP` in `scripts/construct/builder.ts`, used by `ring()`. That a crest uses them and fills its ring: checked on the sheet. |
| 7 | **The animation's phases.** The underdrawing, compass width by compass width, each centre marked with its width's number before its circle is struck; trace what is needed; thicken; rule the lines; fill; finish. Each step's caption names its phase, in English and Japanese. | Built and tested: `src/steps.test.ts`, "goes by the six phases: the underdrawing by compass width, the trace, the thickening, the ruled lines, the fill, the finish" (the order, the centres and width numbers, and the captions), and "marks the centres with a dot and the compass width's number, each on its own switch, and draws line art with the fill off". One thing the test does not hold: the trace follows a shape's whole outline, its straight edges too, before the lines are ruled in phase 4. |
| 8 | **Measure, never copy.** Measuring a crest's proportions from any reference is allowed. Copying a modern drawing's outline or pixels is not. A public-domain book may be vectorised only as a step in measuring, never as the finished drawing. | Checked on the sheet and in review, against the crest's `source` and `docs/provenance.md`. Rule 1's test refuses a traced outline once a crest is off the `PENDING` list. |
| 9 | **The side-by-side gate.** Every crest is put beside a real reference at the same size, and passes only when a stranger could not tell it from the real form, before it ships. | Checked on the sheet: one row per crest, every row looked at. |
| 10 | **A craftsman's construction is followed.** Where a craftsman has published how a crest is constructed (Hatoba's UWAEMON, NHK's デザインあ), ours uses the same circles in the same order. | Checked on the sheet, against the published construction, and credited in the crest's provenance. |
| 11 | **Clockwise, from the top.** Every circle is struck as one clockwise sweep from its top (12 o'clock). A part's circles go by compass width, the smallest first, and those of one width clockwise by their centres; then its ruled lines, each from the centre outwards. Repeated places are visited clockwise, one at a time: a place's guides, then its ink, before the next. It orders the steps only and never changes the finished drawing. | `src/compass.test.ts`, "strikes every circle clockwise from its top, a part's circles by compass width with the smallest first and clockwise by their centres, and then rules its lines from the centre outwards". `src/steps.test.ts`, "turns go round clockwise from the unit, one place at a time: its guides struck, then its shapes inked". Not held by a test: that the unit drawn first is the one nearest the top, and that an outline is traced clockwise (it is traced the way its path is written); checked on the sheet. |
| 12 | **Nothing ever moves.** This is about the crest's pieces; the compass arm is the tool, and follows the pen (a thin line from a circle's centre to the pen while the circle is struck or one of its arcs is inked, the centre's dot bold for as long, gone when the curve ends). A mirrored or turned part is never shown as a copy that flips, slides or rotates into place. It is built where it stands, like the first: its own circles struck, its own lines ruled, then its ink, one place at a time. The data may still say that a part is the first unit mirrored or turned (for the counts, a part shown alone and lineage). | `src/steps.test.ts`, "turns go round clockwise from the unit, one place at a time: its guides struck, then its shapes inked": no style of the animation holds a transform but the compass arm's turn about its centre, and each place's guides are struck there before its ink. "has one compass arm for each circle struck and each arc inked, from that curve's centre, gone when the curve ends, and none with the switch off" holds the arm. `src/compass.test.ts`, "takes every arc from a circle struck in full, and every straight edge from a ruled line", holds each shape against the guides struck at its own place. |

## 作図のルール（日本語）

家紋を描く前、描き直す前、確認する前に、かならず読んでください。どのルールも確かめられる形で書いてあります。横にあるテスト
（`pnpm check` がすべて実行します）で確かめるか、テストでは見えないものは、見比べ用のシートで一つずつ確かめます。

| # | ルール | 確かめ方 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **コンパスと定規だけ。** 家紋は、コンパスの円、描いた円から取った弧、定規の直線だけで作ります。ベジェ曲線、線（ストローク）、オフセットしたパス、短い線をつないだなぞり書きは使いません。 | `src/compass.test.ts` の "is fills outlined in arcs and ruled lines only"。まだ描き直していない家紋は `PENDING` に名前があります。 |
| 2 | **円は一周分を先に描く。** 補助線は円一周か定規の直線で、弧を取る前に描きます。仕上がりの弧はすべて描いた円の上に、まっすぐな辺はすべて引いた直線の上にあります。 | `src/compass.test.ts` の "takes every arc from a circle struck in full, and every straight edge from a ruled line"、`src/steps.test.ts` の "has guides, shapes and ordered steps that draw each exactly once"。定規の直線は、すでに紙の上にある点（中心、または先に描いた円や直線の上の点）から、別のそういう点へ引きます：`src/compass.test.ts` の "rules every line from a point already on the page to another"。まだ守れていない家紋は `LOOSE` に数があり、数は減らすことしかできません。 |
| 3 | **白い線はすき間。** 白は二つの塗りの間に残したすき間で、線ではありません。幅 w の白い線は、半径 r と r + w の弧ではさみます。 | 線がないこと：ルール1のテスト。二つの半径：シートで確認。 |
| 4 | **上と下の重なり。** 上を通る部分があるとき、下の部分の切れ目は、上の部分からずらした円（または直線）ではさみ、その円を先に描きます。 | シートで確認。 |
| 5 | **一つ作ってから回す。** くり返す部分は一つだけ最後まで作り、ほかの位置は「回す」か「裏返す」手順で写します。回す手順では、ほかの位置にも同じ円と直線を描いてから、墨を入れます。 | `src/steps.test.ts` の "turns only a unit already drawn" と "strikes the unit's guides at each other place in the animation"、`src/isolate.test.ts` の "tags every shape and every guide with its part and its unit"。 |
| 6 | **輪は測った太さ。** 輪は直径の0.087、内側の白は0.018、図柄は半径の0.79まで。 | 太さは `scripts/construct/builder.ts` の `RING_WEIGHT` と `RING_GAP`。使っているかどうかはシートで確認。 |
| 7 | **アニメーションの順番。** 下書き（コンパスの幅ごと。円を描く前に、中心に幅の番号つきの印をつける）、必要な線をなぞる、太くする、直線を引く、塗る、仕上げ。手順の説明に、段階の名前が英語と日本語で出ます。 | 作って、テストもあります：`src/steps.test.ts` の "goes by the six phases: …"（順番、中心と幅の番号、説明）と "marks the centres with a dot and the compass width's number, each on its own switch, and draws line art with the fill off"。テストが見ていないこと：なぞる段階では、直線を引く段階より前に、まっすぐな辺もなぞります。 |
| 8 | **測るのはよい、写すのはだめ。** どの資料からでも比率を測ってかまいません。今の人が描いた図の輪郭や画素を写してはいけません。著作権の切れた本は、測るための途中の作業としてだけベクター化できます。 | シートとレビューで確認（家紋の `source` と `docs/provenance.md`）。 |
| 9 | **見比べの関門。** どの家紋も、本物の資料と同じ大きさで並べ、知らない人が見分けられないときだけ合格です。出す前にかならず行います。 | シートで確認（家紋ごとに一行、全部の行を見る）。 |
| 10 | **職人の作図に合わせる。** 職人が作図を公開している家紋（波戸場承龍さんのUWAEMON、NHK「デザインあ」）は、同じ円を同じ順番で使います。 | 公開された作図と見比べてシートで確認し、出典に書きます。 |
| 11 | **上から時計回り。** 円はどれも、いちばん上（12時）から時計回りに一周で描きます。一つの部分の円は、コンパスの幅の小さい順、同じ幅なら中心が時計回りの順です。そのあと直線を、中心から外へ引きます。くり返す位置は時計回りに一か所ずつ回り、その位置の補助線、墨、の順に済ませてから次へ進みます。変わるのは手順だけで、仕上がりの絵は変わりません。 | `src/compass.test.ts` の "strikes every circle clockwise from its top, …"、`src/steps.test.ts` の "turns go round clockwise from the unit, one place at a time: its guides struck, then its shapes inked"。最初に描く一つが上にあること、輪郭を時計回りになぞることは、テストがなく、シートで確認します。 |
| 12 | **何も動かさない。** これは家紋の部品の話です。コンパスの腕は道具なので、ペン先について動きます（円を描く間と弧に墨を入れる間だけ出る、中心からペン先までの細い線。その間は中心の点が太くなり、描き終わると消えます）。裏返した部分や回した部分を、できた形がひっくり返ったり、すべったり、回ったりして入ってくるようには見せません。最初の一つと同じように、その場所で作ります。その場所に円を描き、直線を引き、それから墨を入れ、一か所ずつ進みます。データには「最初の一つを裏返したもの・回したもの」と書いてあってかまいません（数、部品だけの表示、系統のため）。 | `src/steps.test.ts` の "turns go round clockwise from the unit, one place at a time: its guides struck, then its shapes inked"（アニメーションのスタイルで回るのはコンパスの腕だけで、どの位置も補助線が先）と "has one compass arm for each circle struck and each arc inked, …"（コンパスの腕）、`src/compass.test.ts` の "takes every arc from a circle struck in full, and every straight edge from a ruled line"。 |
